She immediately wanted to go for a walk, so we took everyone out. Before long, Biscuit wanted to turn back, so she and I headed home together—but she didn’t make it to the house. She fell and began having a seizure. Just when I thought it had stopped, she had another one.
I carried her into our home, where she seemed to calm down. I worried that I was being silly by taking her to the emergency room, but then she had yet another seizure. We rushed her to the vet, where we received the devastating news that Biscuit had a brain tumor.
The doctor was able to hold off the seizures temporarily, and we brought Loofah to the hospital to say goodbye. Biscuit passed away on the morning of September 8, one day before her gotcha day. She went out as happy as possible, wrapped in a blanket, aloft on a gurney (almost to human level, where she always felt she belonged!), cuddled in our arms - and full of spray cheese, which is probably what she would have preferred if you'd asked her. Before leaving this world, and with perfect comedic timing, she gave a giant snore, loudly and contentedly, like she knew she had done her job as ‘dog’, and did it well.
