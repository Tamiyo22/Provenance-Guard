Biscuit was also a very patient and caring with both Sammy and Ninja. From Sammy stealing her bed, to Ninja deciding that Biscuits tail was her toy for the day, Biscuit loved those two and would just hang out with each one in ways the surprised me. Biscuit and Sammy bonded like two old souls, and Biscuit saw Ninja as her naughty baby sister. 😅
We initially adopted Biscuit to be Matt’s dog, but some of the best memories of my life were spent with just the two of us: going on a beautiful fall hike, walking together on the beach, or sitting beneath a tree while I read a book. I will never forget the magical Thanksgiving hike we had, or her birthday by the lake with fries, turtles, and a very loud swan. Since she had joined us so close to my birthday, I always celebrated together with Biscuit. She loved wearing her birthday hat, and was always so grateful for everything ever given to her. She couldn’t get enough of life or love.
Biscuit was truly the heart of our family, and we will all miss her more than words can express.
Matt and I voted on Halloween this year! We did our usual donations to the Women and Children's shelter in South Austin. The amount of kids in the shelter this year went down to 54!
We also put a sign up in front of our house this year welcoming teenagers for Halloween. And boy did they show up, with some of the coolest costumes. I recommend everyone give this a try. Teenagers aren't adults- let them join in on the fun too. We got a record number of trick-or-treaters - 42 in all! It was a pretty amazing day.
