o...we have a cat again! Her name is Ninja. 🥷 She started showing up in our backyard last October/November. I thought she was a baby skunk at first. 😅
Even though she was incredibly loving I was hesitant to take her in because she would show up with our neighbor's cat. But after seeing her on multiple holidays, and in the cold, we took her in to learn more. We have now had her for a month and it turns out that she does not belong to anyone.
We think that she may have been tossed out of someone's home the way she acts around doors. 😞 She also might be positive for feline leukemia. We're waiting to see if she sheds off her mom's antibodies, which may have caused a false positive. The vet seems certain that she will; however, we are in it to win it with Ninja and we will keep her either way.
It is uncanny how well she fits in our home. She sits on my desk and watches me work. She trusts the dogs and falls asleep on Matt's chest. She seems so grateful to be here and we are grateful to have her. Meet Ninja. 🥰
