Last Wednesday Matt and I brought Sammy into the vet for a checkup, and because he had been extra fussy about his food as of late. We found out that he had fatty liver disease, caused by a fast-growing growth on his liver. The only way to maybe save him would have been through intense hospitalization. We quickly brought Sammy to an emergency vet to get a second opinion. There, the doctors confirmed that his growth was most likely cancer, and that even with chemo and radiation there was no realistic possibility of Sammy living the happy life he once lived, and with his age of almost 18, there was no guarantee that he would even survive the surgery that he would need on his liver.
We brought Sammy home and gave him all the best medicines to make him comfortable for his last days, and gave him his favorite foods. Unusually for a cat, he had always loved the rain, and late that night, after a months-long drought, the skies opened and gave us a huge lightning show to boot. Sammy got the best cuddles, and he purred so loud. I think he knew that these were some of his last days because he snuggled us all so hard, almost as if to say goodbye. He was always such a loving boy.
On Friday afternoon, we had a doctor come to our home from Laps of Love to help Sammy with his next steps. He drifted away peacefully in our arms, loved and honored, surrounded by family.
