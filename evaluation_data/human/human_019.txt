I used to try to downplay my love for Sammy, and his love for me, because society will tell you, “He’s just a cat.” But that cat was my loyal friend and partner. He was my family; at times, my only family. We had a partnership that was not boastful or loud, but quiet and unspoken like a mountain. We were always loyal to each other, and it was never an option to give him up; even during tough times, Sammy came first. We enjoyed so many good times together. We drove across country together and have lived in 3 different states together. I’ll never forget adopting Pickle, and when he growled at me that first week, Sammy jumped right in front of me, to protect and defend me, like a hero ready for battle, because that’s who he was to me, my hero.
