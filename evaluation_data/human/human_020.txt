Next month Sammy would have turned 18 years old. I got him in 2005 (maybe even a bit sooner, all the documents say 2005 though). There was a litter of kittens hanging out in the courtyard of the building I lived in at the time, playing with the children that lived in the apartment on the first floor. One cat ran up to me and meowed. He had such a demanding meow even then, like “Where’s my dinner!?!” haha. The next day I was walking my dog and the very same cat pushed himself right in front of me and my huge Alaskan malamute. I scooped him into my sweatshirt with the intention of finding him a home, but part of me was already picking out a name.
