Biscuit had quite the year. We started it with TPLO surgery and a laryngeal paralysis diagnosis. I became an expert in all things “lar par” and eventually drove Biscuit to Houston for a special new stent procedure. Through it all, Biscuit was so patient with me.
Her procedure took place near where she had been found during Hurricane Harvey, after having been abandoned weeks before the storm. While we were there, we visited Galveston Beach. Biscuit walked along the beach, met other dogs, and was petted and loved on by so many children who told her how beautiful she was. It felt fitting that, during the final weeks of her life, she returned to the place where she had once been neglected and abandoned – only this time, she was deeply loved and cared for.
