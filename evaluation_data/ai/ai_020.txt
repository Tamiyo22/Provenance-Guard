Supporting a senior cat with kidney concerns requires a balance of veterinary guidance and practical daily care. Hydration, appetite, weight trends, and changes in behavior are useful observations to discuss with the veterinary team. Dietary recommendations should be tailored to the individual animal, particularly when other conditions are present. Owners can make the process easier by recording questions before appointments and tracking changes over time. The goal is to maintain comfort and quality of life while making informed decisions together with a veterinarian. Owners may also want to ask which symptoms warrant an urgent appointment and which can be monitored at home. A written record of meals, medications, and observations can make follow-up conversations more productive.
