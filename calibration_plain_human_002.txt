Irish week, day one: soda bread and root vegetable cobble (cheddar biscuit tops)
I kneaded the dough too much, so the bread turned out too dense. The cobbler was my first time ever cooking with turnip and I undercooked them slightly, but overall still pretty good.
Day two: leftover cobble, colcannon (mashed potatoes with cabbage and scallions), plus a much improved sweet soda bread
The colcannon was the best thing so far. A few minutes frying in butter brought out the sweetness of the cabbage, which also added some nice crunch. The new, sweeter bread wasn't far behind either.
