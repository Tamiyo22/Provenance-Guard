I saw Jaren Lewison, from Never Have I Ever, and loved the show idea, so my husband and I gave it a chance this weekend.
We're big fans of the UK Ghosts and What We Do in the Shadows, so I hoped it would be a cute light hearted pick me up, with a little bit of darkness, like those shows, and it can/could be that, it definitely has the potential, but its distracting every time Jessie wipes Phyllis's mind. She's not just some random nosey neighbor like in Bewitched, she is his wife, she has had his children, and it comes across a little date rape-y. My husband took notice of it right away, and with each episode it became a bit more unsettling.
I find Phyllis extremely likeable, but it's hard to watch what is being done to her morally. My husband and I watched almost all of the episodes this weekend, but I am wondering how the writers of the show are going to fix this error on what could have been a really cool show. All of the actors are great, and I don't want Phyllis to go away. But some of the writing choices, like mind wiping Phyllis, the bitch jokes feel like they're being written by a member of a frat house, and not a professional.
